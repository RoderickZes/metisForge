## Learner-generated evidence and portfolios

Evidence created by learners is not automatically part of the upstream Metis Forge project. Screenshots, reports, answers, diagrams, logs and other portfolio artifacts created by a learner remain under that learner's copyright unless the learner explicitly applies a license to them or intentionally contributes them to Metis Forge.

Templates and instructions supplied by Metis Forge inside an `evidence/` directory remain licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. A learner should state separately how their completed evidence may be reused.

## Private modifications

Neither AGPL nor CC BY-SA requires every private modification to be published merely because it was created. Their share-alike obligations are triggered by the activities defined in each license, such as distribution, sharing adaptations and, for modified AGPL software, remote network interaction. These licenses are the strongest standard open licenses suitable for the stated goal, but “always open” is not an absolute obligation to publish every private change.
